### 2. The motor, battery and budget (6:40, 15 min)

**Motor: the 2000 W hub motor.** It's already in a wheel and drives it directly. The Flipsky 6374 would need about a 30:1 gear reduction (Jack's numbers on [#1](https://github.com/Electrium-Mobility/Bakfiets-F26/issues/1)). Ontario limits e-bikes to 500 W and 32 km/h, so the motor controller gets set to 500 W in VESC Tool. That's part of the bench test ([#18](https://github.com/Electrium-Mobility/Bakfiets-F26/issues/18)).

**Battery: Pack 4, the 48 V (12S2P) pack.** It matches the voltage the rest of the bike is designed for (50.4 V full). It has fewer cells than the 12S3P we first planned, so expect less range: roughly 6 Ah instead of 9 Ah. Nobody uses it until it passes the checks in [#21](https://github.com/Electrium-Mobility/Bakfiets-F26/issues/21) with the electrical lead there. Until then, look only: don't charge, plug in or open any pack.

**Budget: about $300 for the term** (from Samantha). We already have most of what we need, so most of it goes to the steering, which is the top mechanical priority. Purchases go through the project lead and Electrium's ordering process. Don't buy parts yourself.
